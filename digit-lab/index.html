Inside KNN

How three neighbors make one guess

  1. 1

    Turn the picture into numbers

    The drawing shrinks to 8 × 8 pixels. Reading their brightness row by row produces a vector with 64 entries.

  2. 2

    Measure every distance

    KNN compares that new vector with all 1,347 labeled vectors in its memory.

  3. 3

    Let the nearest three vote

    Closer pictures receive more influence. The digit with the strongest combined vote becomes KNN’s answer.
